# Bizcraw landing page content and design

## Positioning

Bizcraw is a web scraping and AI sales workspace that connects fresh lead collection, structured scrape results, CRM operations, deals, tasks, quotes, and contracts. The landing page leads with scraping as the acquisition engine and then proves how collected data continues through the full sales workflow.

Primary audience: owners, sales leaders, and lean business-development teams that currently move data between research tools, spreadsheets, CRM records, and document templates.

Primary promise: **Scrape the web. Turn fresh data into sales opportunities.**

## Page narrative

1. Hero: state the scraping outcome and show a real Bizcraw scraping run screen.
2. Capability strip: establish the connected product surface.
3. Problem and outcome: explain why scattered context slows sales work.
4. Workflow: Discover, Organize, Sell, Close.
5. AI agent: explain workspace-grounded answers, previews, permissions, and approvals.
6. Product stories: real screenshots of the source library, connected CRM, and quote execution.
7. Integrations: present the direction without claiming unavailable connectors are live.
8. Security: explain workspace isolation, role checks, and reviewable actions.
9. Dynamic pricing: use the public plan records already managed by the platform.
10. FAQ: answer high-intent product and trust questions.
11. Final CTA and focused footer.

## Visual direction

- Calm, editorial B2B layout with a warm white canvas, near-black green text, and Bizcraw green accents.
- Product evidence appears as UI compositions rather than decorative stock imagery.
- Large, compact typography communicates a mature operational product.
- Borders, spacing, and restrained shadows create hierarchy; color is reserved for actions and meaningful states.
- Responsive design collapses workflows and product stories into readable single-column sections.
- Motion is limited to button feedback and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## SEO and GEO foundation

- One descriptive H1 and a semantic heading hierarchy.
- Specific entity language: lead discovery, CRM, deals, tasks, quotes, contracts, and AI sales workspace.
- Canonical URL and focused Open Graph/Twitter metadata.
- JSON-LD for Organization, WebSite, SoftwareApplication, and FAQPage.
- Public sitemap and crawler rules; authenticated app routes are excluded from indexing.
- Direct, self-contained answers in FAQ content so search engines and answer engines can quote the product accurately.
